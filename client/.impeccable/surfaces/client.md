# Atlas web client (the whole app)

Mode: Operate. Users: a mixed project team (PMs, quantity surveyors, engineers) and non-technical reviewers watching a shared laptop screen in a meeting room, daytime office light. Job: load a folder, ask a question, trust the answer at a glance, check it in one click, export the rows. Constraints: Mantine 9.7 theme object owns every colour, spacing and radius; one brand tone (graphite) plus neutrals; colour for warnings, errors and the file-kind icons only; no hex in components; English or Arabic interface (Arabic fully right to left); light and dark themes, light by default; Arabic answers right to left.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, chat-with-your-documents, executed at the craft of Claude.ai (calm conversation column; a fresh chat is a greeting above the docked question box), Perplexity (numbered sources you can open) and Linear (crisp, dense, quiet chrome; the content on an inset sheet). Grounded: graphite and warm stone, no blue cast, the colours of paper, pencil and a desk's manila folders. It refuses the demo look: gradients, glowing AI sparkles, bubble chat, cards inside cards.

OWN-WORLD: The top bar and sidebar are one continuous stone-neutral chrome field; the conversation sits on it as an inset sheet (page ground, 1px hairline, 8px corners; edge to edge on a phone). One brand tone, graphite (Mantine custom 10-shade palette named `ink`, a near-black at OKLCH hue 85 with almost no chroma; primaryShade 7 in both schemes), spent only on the primary action, focus rings, selection, links, the cited-text highlight, the open file in the tree, the streaming caret, and the ring of the call avatar while Atlas speaks. The primary inverts by scheme: a graphite fill with white text in light, a chalk fill with graphite text in dark. Dark theme: the same roles on warm graphite (chrome darkest, page a step up, raised surfaces a step above), the gray, ink, amber and red shades remapped as roles so every component reads in both; the rendered PDF page stays white paper. Neutral badges for document type and ok status; yellow only for scanned and too-large, red only for corrupt and encrypted, gray for empty and skipped. File kinds carry the colour they are known by on their icon only: PDF red, Excel green, folders two-tone manila like File Explorer's. System UI font stack (Segoe UI carries Arabic on Windows), one family, tabular Western numerals in both languages, ui-monospace only for SQL and tool input. Type scale about 1.15: 12, 13, 15 body, 17, 20, 26. Radii 4/6/8. Hairlines instead of shadows; a soft offset shadow only on modals and menus. The mark is a folded map of three panels, neutral beside the wordmark. The call avatar is a philosopher's bust in graphite line work on white, like marble, in both themes.

STORY: A fresh conversation opens with Atlas's bust on the sheet, which looks up and says its line, "Every answer, with its page.", and hands over the question box, which rises from the sheet's foot. The reviewer sees the project folder as a tree, which files Atlas read and which it could not, and opens any file in the viewer from it. They close the sidebar or drag it wider when they need the room. They ask in plain words, in English or Arabic, from the docked box, and Atlas's face steps back as they type; watch the answer stream; see the exact rows and numbered sources; open a source at its page or rows; expand "How I found this"; export the table. Or they press the phone and ask out loud. They switch language or theme from the top bar and the whole interface follows, mirrored for Arabic.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 52px chrome top bar: the sidebar toggle (Ctrl+B), folded-map mark and "Atlas" at the start; at the end, the last answer's cost and time, the model picker, Clear chat, a hairline, then the language switch (the other language named in its own script) and the light/dark toggle (both move to the sidebar foot on a phone). Chrome sidebar, 300px by default, its edge dragged between 240 and 480px (narrower than 160px closes it; double-click resets; width and open state remembered): folder path field with a folder icon and Load, a compact dropzone, an indexing line while loading, then "Documents" over a folder tree: the loaded folder as a collapsible root with its file count, real subfolders nested, files on a hairline rail under each folder's chevron (kind icon, name, problem badge; pages or sheets and document type under it; the whole row opens the file in the viewer), and "N files skipped" at the foot. Main: the inset sheet holding a centred 760px column, inset on both sides while the sidebar is closed. With nothing asked yet, the opening alone, a little above the middle of the space over the question box: the bust at 156px, Atlas's line and "— Atlas", nothing under it; it plays in about 1.6 s, then the question box rises from the sheet's foot (any click or key finishes it at once), and the face and line step back while a question is in the box. The question box is always docked at the bottom of the sheet; once asked, each turn is the question as a heading-weight line, the streaming answer, the result table, numbered source rows, "Said aloud" under a spoken answer, and a collapsed "How I found this". During a call the call card takes the question box's place.

Signature interaction: the opening, where the bust comes into focus, looks up and says its line as the words rise one by one, its mouth moving with each syllable, and the question box rises out of the sheet's foot to meet it. Then the answer streams in with a graphite caret, and the sources and table settle in below with one 180ms fade-and-rise; clicking a numbered source opens the viewer at that page with the quoted text shaded like pencil, or at the sheet scrolled to the cited rows with those rows tinted. In a call, the bust's mouth moves with Atlas's measured voice while its ring holds the caret's graphite.

Motion grammar: 150 to 200ms ease-out on state changes only (collapse, folder open and close, the sidebar sliding, fade-in of finished parts, hover). Skeleton rows while tables and the tree load. Two exceptions, both approved by the owner on 2026-10-29: the opening of each fresh conversation (focus pull, words rising, then the question box rising 560ms from the sheet's foot; any click or key finishes it; the figure steps back over 180ms while a question is written and returns over 320ms; under reduced motion the still face, the whole line and the box at once), and inside the call avatar, only while a call is open, gentle continuous motion; a still pose per state under reduced motion, and no motion in a hidden tab.
